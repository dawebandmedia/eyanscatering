Full visible image sources were used without changing CDN parameters. No payment screenshots, private chats or customer transaction details are included. No AI or stock photographs are represented as Eyans’ work. Food photos are described broadly without treating visual identification as a verified menu or ingredient list. Years follow the inspected 2026 page timeline; publication dates are not represented as event dates.

The source record establishes publication by the business, not ownership of every pictured item or copyright. Before launch, the owner should approve the copy, wordmark concept, photo permissions, menus, contacts and commercial terms.

## Design and behaviour

The custom visual system uses warm ivory, forest green, light lime and pink, bold sans-serif typography, arch-shaped event images, scrapbook details and a celebration-ticket summary. It intentionally differs from the earlier dark ZION editorial site.

The visitor journey covers services, actual work, food and table details, quote preparation, a three-step planner, FAQs and direct contact. The hero changes to the visitor's chosen occasion. Gallery filters distinguish published event types. Saved inspiration stays in the browser when available and is included as original photo links in an inquiry. The inquiry can be reviewed and edited before the visitor copies it or opens email/Facebook. Nothing is sent automatically. No live booking, payment or backend is connected.

The independent concept does not claim business endorsement, invented ratings, client testimonials, awards or numerical performance claims.
